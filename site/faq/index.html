FAQ

Short answers, no pressure.

Will Reclaim send me reminders?

No. Reclaim never sends notifications. When you open it, it shows what’s due today, and when you close it, it leaves you alone.

What does it cost?

Reclaim is free, with no ads and no account with us. If it helps you and you’d like to support its development, you can chip in on Ko-fi. It’s entirely optional and changes nothing in the app.

Can I add Reclaim to my home screen?

Yes, and it’s worth it: Reclaim then opens full screen from its own icon, like any other app. First open app.reclaim.day, then:

  • iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow; on newer iPhones it’s under the ⋯ button), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  • Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to home screen or Install app.
  • Computer: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar. In Safari on a Mac, choose File › Add to Dock.

You’ll sign in with Google each time you open it, and your log stays in your own Drive as always.

Why is Reclaim free?

Because so many apps like it cost a lot of money, and deciding to change something in your life is hard enough. The help you need shouldn’t be stuck behind a paywall. I’m Chris; I make Reclaim on my own and use it myself, and I’m always glad to hear what would make it better: contact@reclaim.day.

Where is my data?

In your own Google Drive, in a folder called Reclaim. Only you can see it, and we don’t keep a copy. The privacy policy has the details.

Is Reclaim open source?

Yes. The code for the app and this website is public on GitHub under the GNU AGPL 3.0 license, so anyone can check that Reclaim does what it says: no server, no trackers, and your log only goes to your own Drive. The name, logo, website text and screenshots aren’t part of the license.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the Reclaim folder in your Google Drive. To also remove Reclaim’s access to your account, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose Reclaim and select “Delete all connections”. Settings also has a “clear all” option for your live data; your separate backups stay until you delete them.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Settings has a private backup you can download at any time.

Is this medical advice?

No. Reclaim is a tracking tool. It doesn’t diagnose or treat anything, and it’s not a substitute for a doctor or pharmacist. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before changing your medication, and before stopping alcohol suddenly if you drink heavily, because withdrawal can be dangerous. If you’re in crisis, call or text 988 in the US, call 112 in the EU, or see findahelpline.com.
