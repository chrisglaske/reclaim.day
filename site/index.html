Why I built Reclaim. 💛

Hi, I’m Chris. So many apps like this cost a lot of money. Deciding to change something in your life takes courage, and the help you need shouldn’t be stuck behind a paywall. So Reclaim is free, with no ads, and your log stays in your own Drive.

Privacy matters a lot to me, too. Plenty of apps collect what you log, train their models on it, or use it to sell you things. Reclaim has no server that could do any of that: your log goes straight to your own Drive, and I never see it. The code is open on GitHub, so you don’t have to take my word for it.

I use it myself, and I’m always looking for ways to make it better. If there’s something you wish it did, I’d really like to hear from you at contact@reclaim.day.
