What’s new

New things and fixes, newest first. Tap a headline to read more. Reclaim is looked after and still growing.

November 2026

Fixed November 8, 2026

Steadier plans, doses and saving

A quit day you’ve planned ahead now stays on the same date when you travel to another time zone. Changing a weekly limit no longer starts the week’s count over, and a limit you set in the afternoon counts from that day. In Tracking, each past day is now shown against the plan you had that day, so days when you were just tracking are never marked as over.

Changing a medication’s dose or schedule keeps the doses you’ve already noted, and “Taken earlier” only offers times from today. Unmarking a dose or removing a dose note now comes with Undo, and you can clear a weekly reflection by emptying it. Reclaim is also more careful when saving to your Drive: a reply that gets lost on a slow connection no longer leaves a second copy of your data, and a change that couldn’t be saved is no longer passed over in silence.

New November 2, 2026

A getting-started card, notes to yourself and a pill count

New to Reclaim? A small Getting started card on Today gathers two easy first steps, a first check-in and a first moment or dose, and ticks them off by itself. Close it whenever you like. It also goes by itself after a week.

If you’ve been away for a few days, Today now says a quiet welcome back with one easy next step. Evening check-ins have an optional note for tomorrow morning: it shows on Today the next day, and it stays in your Journal.

For a medication you take on a schedule, you can enter how many pills you have left in Settings. Reclaim counts down as you note doses and mentions, as an estimate, when about a week is left. Clear the box to turn it off. And if something ever goes wrong in the app, a calm note now offers to copy the details so you can send them to contact@reclaim.day. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Fixed November 2, 2026

Smoother first days, and fewer surprises

A medication you add partway through the day no longer shows that morning’s dose as late. A plan you start in the afternoon now counts its first day toward your savings. Tapping Save twice in a check-in no longer causes an error, and long names wrap instead of running off narrow screens.

New November 1, 2026

Quicker logging, and past days that are easy to fix

Check-ins are shorter: choose Save now whenever you’ve said enough, with no Done screen after. If you close one partway through, what you entered is still there when you come back. Noting a moment starts from the kind and amount you chose last time, and Earlier has quick choices for the time. When several doses are due, one button notes them all, with Undo if you need it.

Tap anything you’ve logged in Today or the Journal to change it, and add moments and waves to a past day in the Journal. The week boxes in Tracking, the heat cells and dose dots in Insights, and the chips on Today now open that day in your Journal, and the stops on Today’s timeline can be tapped too. Insights also shows how this week compares with last week.

Fixed November 1, 2026

A cleaner fit on small screens

Labels on Tracking, Quick log and check-ins no longer get squeezed or cut off on narrow phones. Chips sit properly centered, and the buttons at the bottom of a sheet stay whole. Pages and sheets also stay put now when a swipe up or down drifts sideways.

October 2026

Fixed October 31, 2026

Smoother appointments, easier tapping and a steadier home page

Appointments: picking a medication while planning a visit no longer fills its note with the word “undefined”, and any note saved that way is cleared. Opening an appointment now works when your last visit was over a year ago, a visit you didn’t go to no longer counts as your last one, and dose times follow your 12 or 24-hour setting.

In the app: picking a day in Look back, or getting changes from another device, no longer redraws the whole page, and you keep your place. The mood and Journal buttons are easier to tap, and the mood row now fits narrow phones. Just track shows your check-ins in Insights instead of an empty limit, Escape always closes a check-in, and if loading takes a long time, you can try again.

On this website: the home page now settles on one section at a time, on every screen size.

New October 30, 2026

Plan, prepare for and keep track of appointments

Appointments now has its own place in the menu, with what’s coming up, what’s passed and your doctors. Plan a visit with its date, doctor, what it’s for and any medications to talk about, then go through your questions and what’s changed. Questions you don’t get to carry over to the next one.

Once you’re prepared, the visit becomes one page with your questions and summary. It shows in When you’re ready on the day and in your Journal. Write down the answers you get, keep what came of it, or note that you didn’t go. You can add a visit to your calendar with only the details you choose, and show a doctor’s number in Need support.

Look back, in Insights, replaces the appointment summary for any dates you choose, with a calendar of the period to open a single day.

New October 30, 2026

Note how your body feels

Check-ins have a new optional step for things like a headache, stomach issues or feeling tired, and Other lets you write it in your own words. It shows in your Journal and in your summary, with the dates each one was noted. If a past day has no check-ins, the Journal now offers one short day check-in instead of three to fill in.

New October 30, 2026

Easier doses, and a few smaller things

If scheduled doses from the last 7 days have no note, Today and Tracking show a short line that opens them all in one list. Quick log now just notes a dose before its time, and asks whether you took it just now or earlier once its time has passed.

Pick how dates and times read in Settings › Look & feel or during Guided setup: your device’s format, US or International. The fields you pick a date or time from still follow your device. On phones, the menu now has five even tabs, with Quick log as a floating button.

New October 28, 2026

Reclaim is open source

All of Reclaim’s code, the app and this website, is now public on GitHub. You can see for yourself that your log only goes to your own Google Drive, and nowhere else.

Fixed October 28, 2026

Signing in again on a computer brings in your latest

When your Google sign-in ends on a computer and you sign in again, changes from your other devices now show up right away, without switching tabs first.

Fixed October 27, 2026

The “Tricky window” label is easy to read

On narrower screens the tallest bar in “When urges show up” could cover the “Tricky window” label. The bars now always fit inside the chart.

New October 26, 2026

Edit or remove anything in your Journal

Every entry in a day, whether a moment, a wave you rode out or a dose note, now has Edit and Remove buttons. Moments reopen the form you filled in. Waves and dose notes let you change when they happened. Removing one can be undone right away.

New October 26, 2026

Delete medications you no longer take

Archived medications now have a Deleted list in Settings. You can restore one, or delete it for good. The appointment summary also lets you pick which medications to include.

Fixed October 26, 2026

Changes from your other devices show up

If you use Reclaim on a phone and a computer, a change made on one now appears on the other when you come back to it, without starting over.

New October 25, 2026

A new default look: Zine

Cream, navy and cobalt, with the look from reclaim.day. Pine, Ocean, Graphite and Night are still there under Look & feel in Settings.

New October 25, 2026

Add Reclaim to your home screen

Reclaim can now open full screen from its own icon, like any other app. Steps for your phone or computer are in the FAQ.

New October 24, 2026

Reclaim has a new home

Reclaim now lives at app.reclaim.day. You sign in with Google, and what you save stays in your own Google Drive, in a folder only you can see.

New October 24, 2026

Not every day? That’s fine

Set a limit per day, for chosen days, or as a total for the week. Or choose Just track, with no limit at all.

New October 24, 2026

An appointment summary

See what changed since your last appointment, with medication, habits, mood, cravings and sleep, plus questions to ask. You can copy it or print it to bring along.

Fixed October 24, 2026

Today no longer wobbles on phones

The Today screen used to slide a few pixels sideways while you scrolled. It stays put now.
